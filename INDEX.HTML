Our Story

A Corner That
Feels Like Home

Roast & Ritual was born in 2019 from a small corner in Chh. Sambhajinagar — a city with a soul as rich as its history. Our founder Soham started with one espresso machine, one grinder, and an obsession with making the perfect cup.

We source our beans from Coorg and Chikmagalur, roasted fresh every week by a small family roastery we've worked with since day one. Every drink you order here is made by hand, with time and intention.

We believe a café is more than coffee — it's where ideas are born, friendships deepen, and the world slows down just enough.
